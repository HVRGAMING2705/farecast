✈️ FareCast — Flight Fare Predictor

A 2nd-year B.Tech AI/ML project by Vikranth Reddy. A ridge regression model trained on 600 synthetic flight bookings predicts a ticket fare from airline tier, stops, duration, days booked before departure, route type and travel class. 5-fold CV: R² = 0.977, MAE = ₹1,284 vs a mean-guess baseline MAE of ₹8,349 (mean fare ₹27,599).

How it works: your six numbers are standardised (subtract the training mean, divide by the standard deviation), multiplied by the model's learned weights, and summed with a bias to give the fare in rupees; predictions are floored at ₹1,500. The exact same weights run in this page and in the Python training script (train.py), so the browser result matches the trained model.

Try it: a budget non-stop 2-hour short domestic economy flight, booked 30 days early → about ₹6,734. A premium non-stop 9-hour international business flight, booked 3 days early → about ₹44,607. A mid-range 1-stop 5.5-hour long domestic economy flight, booked 45 days early → about ₹18,078.

Honest note: this model was trained on synthetic bookings generated from simple fare logic, so it is a classroom demo — real fares also depend on the exact route, season, demand and the airline's dynamic pricing, which six numbers can't capture.
